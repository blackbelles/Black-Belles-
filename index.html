Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Mission

BBE seeks to foster self-esteem, self-confidence, determination and persistence in young women while encouraging academic excellence, community engagement and health through dance.

Vision

BBE is a diverse dance program including Hip Hop, Jazz, African, Majorette and Modern, with various instructors, gymnastics and technique training, state and national competitions, and community events such as parades and festivals.

Student + Parent Requirements

The BBE Standard

01

Maintain at least a 3.0 average and good conduct.

02

No profanity, fighting or disruptive behavior. Noncompliance may lead to suspension or dismissal.

03

Parents communicate schedule conflicts. Practice schedules are posted in the Facebook group monthly. Too many missed practices or performances can lead to suspension or dismissal.

04

No parents in practices. Students must be dropped off and picked up on time.

05

Students and parents are required to participate in fundraisers, recitals, competitions, community functions, practices and other team activities.

06

Uniform requirements and purchases are announced in the Facebook group. Dance shoes, knee pads and other required items must be obtained.

07

Monthly tuition is $65, due by the 15th. Each late week adds $5. After two missed payments without arrangements, the child cannot compete. Communicate if financial issues arise.

08

A parent, family member or friend age 18+ must accompany the child to performances and competitions.

09

A scholarship is for one year unless a longer period is approved.

10

Hard work and fun go together; the team should not feel like a job.
